TITLE
Low-rank Grothendieck rings: an AI-assisted classification project, constructions, and open problems

REPOSITORY OVERVIEW DATE
29 October 2026

ABSTRACT
We present a public AI-generated research project on fusion rings and their categorifications over $\mathbb C$, with data extending through rank twelve. The project reports unrestricted Grothendieck-ring classifications in ranks two, three, and four, a complete multiplicity-free classification through rank twelve, and classifications or partial classifications at higher multiplicities. The released census contains $1{,}434{,}042$ rings in ranks three through twelve; six categorification questions remain open within its stated bounds. The resources include research manuscripts, categorical models, exclusion arguments, verification code, and an interactive atlas. One categorical model per ring suffices for our classification problem; classifying all realizing categories up to equivalence is a further task. We summarize the current scope, compare it with AnyonWiki, and link to the online constructions catalogue and unresolved cases. The project's original research content was generated by AI under human direction; independent human verification remains pending.
